Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about wildlife, national parks, safari experiences and conservation.

What is WILDPLANET?

WILDPLANET is a wildlife exploration platform created to spread awareness about different animal species, their habitats and wildlife conservation.

What can I explore on this website?

You can explore different animal species, natural habitats, famous national parks of India, wildlife photographs, videos and interesting wildlife facts.

Can I book a wildlife safari through this website?

Our website provides a safari booking information section where visitors can explore safari experiences and fill out the booking form. Actual booking confirmation depends on the respective park or authorized operator.

Which national parks are featured here?

We feature famous Indian national parks such as Jim Corbett, Ranthambore, Kaziranga, Kanha and other popular wildlife destinations.

Why is wildlife conservation important?

Wildlife conservation helps protect endangered species, maintain ecological balance and preserve natural habitats for future generations.

How can I contribute to wildlife protection?

You can help by protecting natural habitats, avoiding plastic pollution, spreading awareness and supporting responsible wildlife conservation activities.

Is WILDPLANET free to explore?

Yes! Visitors can freely explore our wildlife information, animal galleries, educational content and conservation resources.
