EXPLORE 6 MAJOR HABITATES 🏞️

Discover six unique habitats, each filled with extraordinary wildlife, diverse ecosystems, and fascinating natural wonders.

FORESTS

Forests are rich natural habitats filled with tall trees, dense vegetation, and diverse wildlife. They provide food, shelter, and protection to animals such as tigers, elephants, deer, monkeys, and countless bird species.

Wildlife Facts:

The Bengal tiger uses dense forests for shelter, hunting, and camouflage.
Sloths spend most of their lives living in the trees of tropical rainforests.

Learn More ➥

GRASSLANDS

Grasslands are wide open habitats covered mainly with grasses and small plants, with very few trees. They provide food and shelter to animals such as lions, zebras, giraffes, elephants, and antelopes.

Wildlife Facts:

Grasslands are home to some of the fastest land animals, including cheetahs.
Zebras use their stripes as a natural form of camouflage in herds.

Learn More ➥

DESERTS

Deserts are dry habitats that receive very little rainfall and experience extreme temperatures. Despite harsh conditions, they are home to specially adapted animals such as camels, foxes, snakes, lizards, and scorpions.

Wildlife Facts:

Camels can survive for long periods without drinking water.
Fennec foxes use their large ears to release body heat and stay cool.

🌿 INDIA'S INCREDIBLE HABITATS 🌿

Our country is blessed with diverse ecosystems and rich wildlife.

SUNDARBANS

The Sundarbans is a remarkable mangrove ecosystem and UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is home to the Royal Bengal Tiger, estuarine crocodiles and hundreds of bird species.

KAZIRANGA

Famous for the greater one-horned rhinoceros, its grasslands and wetlands also support elephants, tigers, deer and many other species.

HIMALAYAS

Majestic mountains with alpine meadows, forests and glaciers. Home to snow leopards, musk deer, Himalayan tahr and red pandas.

RANN OF KUTCH

A unique seasonal salt marsh that blooms with life during the monsoon. It supports flamingos, wild asses and migratory birds.

WHY HABITATS MATTER

🌿 Food & Water – Provide essential resources for all living beings.

🌿 Natural Shelter – Offer protection, breeding and resting places.

🌿 Biodiversity – Support thousands of plant and animal species.

🌿 Ecological Balance – Maintain food chains and ecosystems.

🌿 Climate Regulation – Forests and oceans help regulate climate.

🌿 Soil & Water Protection – Prevent erosion and support clean water.

THREATS TO WILDLIFE HABITATS

🔴 Deforestation – Loss of forests leads to loss of wildlife homes.

🔴 Climate Change – Disrupts ecosystems and weather patterns.

🔴 Pollution – Harms water, soil and air quality.

🔴 Urbanization – Cities and roads fragment natural habitats.

🔴 Overexploitation – Overhunting and illegal trade threaten species.

AMAZING NATURE FACTS

🌳 The Amazon Rainforest is the world's largest tropical rainforest.

🐠 Coral reefs are often called the rainforests of the sea.

🌿 Mangroves help protect coastal areas from erosion.

🏜️ Deserts may look empty, but they are full of life adapted to extreme conditions.
